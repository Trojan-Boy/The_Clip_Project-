# Idea Factory

Idea Factory is a fully automated AI startup pipeline organized into four operational layers:

**Layer 1 — Intelligence:** Monitors the internet 24/7. Finds, collects, and filters the best startup ideas from YC, Hacker News, and startup media.

**Layer 2 — Decision:** Acts as your internal investment committee. Scores ideas, kills bad ones fast, and greenlits the winners.

**Layer 3 — Build:** Your fully automated product studio. Takes the winning idea and ships a working app with design specs.

**Layer 4 — Launch:** Gets your product in front of users. Generates landing pages, marketing copy, and growth strategy automatically.

Work flows sequentially through the layers: Intelligence scouts discover ideas → Decision committee evaluates and greenlits → Build team constructs the MVP → Launch team ships it to users.
