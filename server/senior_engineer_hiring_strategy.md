## Phase 3: Hiring Strategy & Timeline
**POL-9: Develop Senior Engineer hiring strategy and timeline**

### Recruitment Channels
- [ ] Job boards (LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor)
- [ ] Developer communities (GitHub, Stack Overflow)
- [ ] Employee referral program
- [ ] Technical recruitment agencies
- [ ] University partnerships and internships

### Interview Process
- **Stage 1: Initial Screening**
  - Resume review
  - Phone screening (30-45 minutes)

- **Stage 2: Technical Assessment**
  - Coding challenge (2 hours)
  - System design exercise (1 hour)
  - Technical interview (1 hour)

- **Stage 3: Final Interview**
  - Team fit interview (1 hour)
  - Leadership interview (1 hour)
  - Values alignment discussion

### Timeline
- **Week 1-2:** Preparation and job posting
- **Week 3-4:** Application review and initial screening
- **Week 5-6:** Technical assessments
- **Week 7:** Final interviews
- **Week 8:** Offer negotiation and acceptance

### Success Metrics
- [ ] Time-to-hire: 6-8 weeks
- [ ] Candidate experience score: 4.5/5
- [ ] Quality of hire: Demonstrated through performance review
- [ ] Diversity and inclusion goals
