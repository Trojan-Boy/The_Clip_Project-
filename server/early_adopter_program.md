# Early Adopter Program (EAP)
## Program Goals:
* Obtain feedback on product features and usability.
* Build a community of early advocates.
* Gather testimonials and case studies.
* Achieve 100 active customers.

## Participant Benefits:
* **Exclusive Access:** Early access to new features and product updates.
* **Direct Influence:** Opportunity to directly influence product roadmap through dedicated feedback channels.
* **Premium Support:** Expedited and personalized support.
* **Recognition:** Featured as an early adopter on our website/marketing materials (optional).
* **Discounted/Free Access:** Special pricing or free access for a limited period.

## Participant Requirements:
* Must be willing to provide regular, constructive feedback.
* Commit to using the product for a minimum duration (e.g., 3 months).
* Participate in occasional interviews or surveys.
* Allow us to use their company logo (with permission).

## Our Responsibilities:
* Provide timely support and address feedback.
* Communicate roadmap updates and upcoming features.
* Acknowledge and act on valuable feedback.
* Ensure data privacy and security.

## Onboarding Process:
1. Application and qualification.
2. Welcome kit with program details and access instructions.
3. Introduction to feedback channels.
4. Initial product walkthrough/training (if needed).

## Exit Criteria for EAP:
* Successful completion of the defined participation period.
* Product reaches general availability.
* Participant no longer meets program requirements.
