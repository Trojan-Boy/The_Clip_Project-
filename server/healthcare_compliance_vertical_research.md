## Part 1: Healthcare Compliance Requirements Analysis

### 1.1 Core Regulatory Framework

#### HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act)
```
Protected Health Information (PHI) Requirements:
├── Privacy Rule: PHI use and disclosure limitations
├── Security Rule: Administrative, physical, technical safeguards
├── Breach Notification Rule: 60-day reporting requirement
└── Enforcement Rule: Civil/criminal penalties

Key Components:
- Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) required
- Minimum Necessary Standard for PHI access
- Risk Analysis & Management mandatory
- Patient Rights (access, amendment, accounting)
```

#### TEFCA (Trusted Exchange Framework and Common Agreement)
- **Effective date**: 2025 implementation deadlines
- **Purpose**: Nationwide healthcare data exchange framework
- **Requirements**:
  - Standardized API connectivity (FHIR R4)
  - Participant directory management
  - Audit logging and monitoring
  - Data provenance tracking
- **Impact**: All digital health platforms must comply for interoperability

### 1.2 Additional Healthcare Regulations

#### FDA Regulations for Digital Health
- **Software as Medical Device (SaMD)**: AI/ML algorithms for diagnosis/treatment
- **Digital Health Pre-Cert Program**: Streamlined review for qualified developers
- **Clinical Decision Support Software**: Risk-based classification

#### State-Specific Requirements
- **California Confidentiality of Medical Information Act (CMIA)**
- **Texas Medical Privacy Act**
- **New York SHIELD Act** (health data specific provisions)

#### International Healthcare Regulations
- **EU Medical Device Regulation (MDR)**
- **UK NHS Digital Standards**
- **Canada PIPEDA healthcare amendments**
