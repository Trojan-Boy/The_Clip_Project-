## Prioritization Framework

### Value vs. Effort Matrix
- **High Value, Low Effort:** Immediate priority
- **High Value, High Effort:** Strategic investment
- **Low Value, Low Effort:** Quick wins
- **Low Value, High Effort:** Defer or eliminate

### Decision Criteria
1. **Business Impact:** Revenue generation, cost reduction, competitive advantage
2. **User Value:** User experience improvement, feature adoption potential
3. **Technical Debt:** Critical fixes, infrastructure improvements
4. **Market Timing:** Competitive positioning, market window of opportunity
5. **Resource Availability:** Team capacity, budget constraints
