#### **ComplianceGPT** (Now "Regula" - Pivot & Rebrand)
- **Status:** Launched Q4 2025, rebranded to "Regula" March 2026
- **What Happened:** Initial LLM-only approach failed - too many hallucinations in compliance advice
- **Current Model:** Human-in-the-loop AI + certified compliance experts reviewing all outputs

- **Status:** Still in beta, enterprise focus continues to dominate

- **Current Offering:** Basic SMB package at $199/month (very limited features)

- **Assessment:** LOW-MEDIUM threat - not serious about SMB market

- **Status:** Acquired by OneTrust for $45M in Q1 2026
- **What Happened:** Strong tech, weak sales - OneTrust bought for regulatory change detection IP
- **Integration:** Being rolled into OneTrust platform, not available as standalone
- **Assessment:** NEUTRAL - technology absorbed by enterprise player

## Part 2: 2026 AI Technology Trends Assessment

### 2.1 Evolution from Generative AI to Agentic AI

#### **Phase 1 (2023-2024): Document AI**
- Text generation, document analysis
- Basic Q&A on regulations
- Limited automation

#### **Phase 2 (2025): Workflow AI**  
- Process automation
- Regulatory change monitoring
- Integration with business apps

#### **Phase 3 (2026): Agentic AI** (CURRENT FRONTIER)
- Autonomous compliance agents
- Multi-step reasoning and execution
- Cross-platform task completion
- Predictive compliance risk modeling

### 2.2 Emerging Agentic AI Capabilities for Compliance

#### **Autonomous Audit Agents**
- **Capability:** Self-directed evidence collection across multiple systems
- **Example:** Agent logs into AWS, Slack, Jira automatically to gather SOC 2 evidence
- **Current Players:** AuditFlow (partial), internal R&D at larger players
- **Our Gap:** Basic document collection vs full autonomy

#### **Predictive Compliance Risk Engines**
- **Capability:** Machine learning models predicting regulatory changes 3-6 months out
- **Data Sources:** Legislative drafts, enforcement patterns, geopolitical factors
- **Sophistication:** Still early (60-70% accuracy)
- **Opportunity:** First to market with >80% accuracy could dominate

#### **Multimodal Evidence Processing**
- **Capability:** Video, audio, screenshots as audit evidence
- **Use Case:** Remote workforce compliance monitoring
- **Technology:** Computer vision + NLP for compliance validation
- **Adoption:** Minimal currently - blue ocean opportunity

### 2.3 Technology Adoption Curve

**Early Adopters (2026):** 
- Predictive risk modeling (15% adoption)
- Basic agentic features (10% adoption)

**Early Majority (2027-2028 projected):**
- Autonomous audit agents (40% projected)
- Multimodal evidence (35% projected)

**Late Majority (2029+ projected):**
- Full agentic compliance (70%+ projected)
