### What This Means for Product:

2. **Proactive Guidance**: Tell them what to do, not just what's required
3. **Integration Focus**: Work within their existing tools
4. **Simplicity**: Hide complexity, show clarity

## The Three Biggest Risks (and Mitigation):

### 1. **AI Liability Risk**


**Risk**: Wrong compliance advice leads to customer fines
**Mitigation**: 
- Human review for critical recommendations
- Clear disclaimer of AI advisory role
- Professional services layer for complex cases
- Errors & omissions insurance

### 2. **Competitive Response Risk**
**Risk**: Enterprise players create SMB offerings
**Mitigation**:
- Focus on SMB user experience (their weakness)
- Build AI advantage (their legacy tech handicap)
- Create partner ecosystem (their direct sales bias)
- Maintain startup speed (their bureaucratic slowness)

### 3. **Market Education Risk**
**Risk**: SMBs don't understand their compliance needs
**Mitigation**:
- Education-first marketing approach
- Free risk assessment to demonstrate need
- Focus on regulated industries first
- Build trust through transparency and results
