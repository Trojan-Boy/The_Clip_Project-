# Partnership Criteria for Paperclip

To effectively explore partnership opportunities and expand Paperclip's reach, the following criteria will be used to identify complementary products and services:

1.  **Audience Overlap:**
    *   **Description:** Partners should target a similar customer base as Paperclip, but ideally without direct competition. This ensures that the partnership can introduce Paperclip to new, relevant users.
    *   **Considerations:** Look for users in creative, development, or business automation sectors.

2.  **Value Proposition Alignment:**
    *   **Description:** The partner's product or service should enhance or extend Paperclip's core offerings. This could be by providing tools for project management, communication, design, or specialized development kits that complement Paperclip's automation capabilities.
    *   **Examples:** Tools for project management (e.g., Jira, Asana), communication (e.g., Slack, Microsoft Teams), design (e.g., Figma), or code generation/management.

3.  **Technical Compatibility/Integration Potential:**
    *   **Description:** Ideally, there should be a clear path for technical integration between Paperclip and the partner's platform, adding value to users of both products. This could be through APIs, plugins, or shared data formats.
    *   **Considerations:** Ease of integration, developer support, existing API documentation.

4.  **Brand Synergy:**
    *   **Description:** The partner's brand values, reputation, and quality should align with Paperclip's to maintain trust and credibility.
    *   **Considerations:** Shared commitment to innovation, user-centricity, and reliability.

5.  **Mutual Growth Opportunity:**
    *   **Description:** The partnership should offer clear benefits to both parties, such as increased user acquisition, improved user retention, new revenue streams, or expanded market reach.
    *   **Metrics:** Potential for joint marketing, co-selling, or shared KPIs.

6.  **Product Stage/Maturity:**
    *   **Description:** Partners should ideally be stable and established enough to ensure a reliable and long-term partnership, but could also be innovative startups offering unique value.
    *   **Considerations:** Risk assessment, scalability of partnership.
