# ComplyFlow Key Metrics and KPIs

To effectively analyze funnel performance and improve user acquisition for ComplyFlow, the following Key Metrics and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) will be tracked across the user journey:

## 1. Awareness/Reach
*   **Website Traffic:** Total unique visitors and page views to ComplyFlow's website and landing pages. (Metric)
*   **Impressions & Reach:** For digital marketing campaigns (e.g., ads, social media posts). (Metric)
*   **Brand Mentions:** Tracking mentions across social media, news, and industry forums. (Metric)

## 2. Acquisition
*   **New Sign-ups/Registrations:** Number of new user accounts created (including free trials and paid subscriptions). (KPI)
*   **Website Visitor to Sign-up Conversion Rate:** Percentage of website visitors who complete the sign-up process. (KPI)
*   **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Total sales and marketing spend divided by the number of new customers acquired in a given period. (KPI)
*   **Lead-to-Opportunity Conversion Rate:** For B2B sales, the percentage of marketing-qualified leads that become sales opportunities. (KPI)
*   **Opportunity-to-Customer Conversion Rate:** For B2B sales, the percentage of sales opportunities that close as new customers. (KPI)

## 3. Activation
*   **First Use Completion Rate:** Percentage of new users who complete a crucial first action, such as setting up their first compliance rule, integrating a data source, or generating an initial report. (KPI)
*   **Time to First Value (TTFV):** The average time it takes for a new user to experience the core benefit or achieve their primary goal with ComplyFlow. (KPI)
*   **Onboarding Completion Rate:** Percentage of users who successfully navigate and complete the guided onboarding flow. (KPI)

## 4. Retention
*   **Customer Churn Rate:** The percentage of customers who cancel their subscription or stop using ComplyFlow over a defined period (e.g., monthly, quarterly). (KPI)
*   **Customer Retention Rate:** The percentage of customers who continue their subscription over a defined period. (KPI)
*   **Daily/Weekly/Monthly Active Users (DAU/WAU/MAU):** The number of unique users actively engaging with the platform on a daily, weekly, or monthly basis. (Metric/KPI)
*   **Feature Adoption/Usage:** Tracking the usage patterns of key ComplyFlow features to understand engagement depth and identify underutilized areas. (Metric/KPI)

## 5. Revenue
*   **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU):** The average revenue generated by each active user over a specific period. (KPI)
*   **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):** The predicted total revenue that a customer will generate throughout their relationship with ComplyFlow. (KPI)
*   **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) / Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) Growth Rate:** The rate at which ComplyFlow's recurring revenue is increasing. (KPI)

## 6. Referral/Advocacy
*   **Net Promoter Score (NPS):** A measure of customer loyalty and satisfaction, indicating their willingness to recommend ComplyFlow to others. (KPI)
*   **Referral Rate:** The percentage of new customers acquired through existing customer referrals. (KPI)
