# Growth-Oriented MVPs for Regulatory Knowledge Graph

## MVP 1: Basic Search & Alerting
### Features:
- Keyword search for regulations
- Filter by jurisdiction and industry
- Save searches
- Email alerts for changes to saved regulations or new relevant regulations

### Growth Rationale:
- Addresses immediate pain point of finding and tracking regulations.
- High utility for a broad range of users (legal, compliance, business development).
- Easy to demonstrate value quickly.
- Promotes recurring usage through alerts.

## MVP 2: Compliance Checklist Generator
### Features:
- User input for industry, jurisdiction, and business type.
- Generates a basic, tailored compliance checklist.
- Links directly to relevant regulations within the knowledge graph.

### Growth Rationale:
- Simplifies complex compliance processes, saving users time and effort.
- Provides actionable guidance, building trust and authority.
- Attracts users new to compliance or those expanding into new areas.

## MVP 3: Regulatory Impact Analyzer (Simple)
### Features:
- Users can upload documents (e.g., policy proposals, business plans).
- AI-powered analysis to identify potentially impacted regulations.
- Highlights specific sections of documents relevant to regulations.

### Growth Rationale:
- Offers proactive risk management and strategic insights.
- Appeals to users in decision-making roles (e.g., legal counsel, business strategists).
- Showcases advanced capabilities of the knowledge graph, attracting early adopters seeking innovative solutions.
