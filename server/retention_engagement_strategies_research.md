## 2. Key Metrics for Retention and Engagement

### 2.1. Retention Metrics

*   **Customer Retention Rate:** The percentage of customers who continue to subscribe or use your service over a given period.
    *   *Formula:* `((Customers at End of Period - New Customers Acquired During Period) / Customers at Start of Period) * 100`
*   **Churn Rate:** The percentage of customers who stop using your service over a given period.
    *   *Formula:* `(Number of Churns / Customers at Start of Period) * 100`
*   **Revenue Churn Rate:** The percentage of revenue lost from existing customers over a given period.
    *   *Formula:* `(MRR Churn / Starting MRR) * 100`
*   **Cohort Analysis:** Analyzing the behavior of groups of customers who signed up around the same time to understand retention trends over time.

### 2.2. Engagement Metrics

*   **Daily/Weekly/Monthly Active Users (DAU/WAU/MAU):** The number of unique users who interact with your product on a daily, weekly, or monthly basis. These indicate how frequently users are returning.
*   **Feature Usage:** Tracking which features users are interacting with and how often. This helps identify sticky features and areas for improvement.
*   **Time Spent in Product:** The average amount of time users spend actively using the platform. Longer times can indicate deeper engagement, depending on the product's nature.
*   **Session Duration/Frequency:** How long individual user sessions last and how often users log in.
*   **Net Promoter Score (NPS):** A measure of customer loyalty and satisfaction, indicating how likely customers are to recommend your product to others. While not a direct engagement metric, it's a strong indicator of overall satisfaction which contributes to engagement and retention.
*   **Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV):** The total revenue a business can reasonably expect from a single customer account throughout their relationship. This is a lagging indicator but ties directly to successful retention.
