# Key Metrics and KPIs for User Acquisition Funnel Optimization

## Issue: IDE-177 - Define Key Metrics and KPIs (Sub-issue 1.2)

### Objective:
Establish clear, measurable metrics to track the success of optimization efforts for the user acquisition funnel.

### Primary Conversion Goals:

*   **Trial Sign-ups (or equivalent first major commitment):** The number of users who initiate and complete a free trial or similar initial product engagement.
    *   **Baseline:** 280 (from an initial 10,000 visitors)
*   **Active Users (post-onboarding):** Users who actively use the product within a defined period after account creation/trial start.
    *   **Baseline (Hypothetical):** 140 (assuming a 50% activation rate from Trial Sign-ups)

### Secondary Metrics/Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

*   **Landing Page Conversion Rate (to 'Add to Cart' / 'Start Free Trial'):** Percentage of visitors to a key landing page who proceed to the next step.
    *   **Baseline:** 15%
*   **Signup Form Completion Rate:** Percentage of users who start the signup form and successfully complete it.
    *   **Baseline:** 40%
*   **Mobile Conversion Rate (Overall):** Overall conversion rate specifically for mobile users, from visitor to trial sign-up.
    *   **Baseline:** 25%
*   **Bounce Rate (Key Landing Pages):** Percentage of visitors who leave the site after viewing only one page on critical landing pages.
    *   **Baseline:** 45% (Overall average, to be refined per page)
*   **Time on Page (Key Landing Pages):** Average time users spend on critical product/service pages.
    *   **Baseline:** 1:30 min (Overall average, to be refined per page)
*   **Click-Through Rate (CTAs):** Effectiveness of Calls-to-Action on crucial funnel pages.
    *   **Baseline:** *To be determined post-implementation, as specific CTA data was not explicitly in the simulated analysis.*

### Notes:
*   Baseline values are derived from the hypothetical funnel performance analysis conducted in IDE-176.
*   Specific baselines for individual CTAs will be established once granular data is available or as part of A/B testing.
*   These metrics and KPIs will be regularly monitored to assess the impact of optimization efforts outlined in the Growth Agent Plan (`growth_agent_plan.md`).
